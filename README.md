Entry for the make.fun bounty [Make the AI judge feel awe](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

The program writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is a single quiet scene: a meteor shower over a field at night, a man lying in warm grass counting the meteors as they thin out, the Milky Way turning overhead, and him standing at the end, steady and ready to move on. Plain words, no instructions, nothing to do but watch the sky widen.

Run it with `node main.ts` (Node 22). It writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or `/output`. Deterministic, no network, no randomness.
